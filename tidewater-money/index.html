Tidewater Money

Know what renews, what's due and what quietly went up. Tidewater Money keeps your subscriptions and bills in one calm place, nudges you before anything charges, and points out the things worth a second look. It has no internet permission, so your numbers never leave the phone.

Personal project · Not publicly available yet

What it does

An original, home-made money helper focused on the things that repeat. You add what you pay for, or bring in a statement file, and it does the remembering.

Subscriptions, all in one list

Amount, how often (weekly, monthly, yearly or a custom schedule like every 2 weeks), next charge, status and a payment label. See what it all costs per month and per year.

Bills that vary

Utilities, insurance and card bills with a usual range, autopay or paid by hand, and a Mark paid button that records the payment and moves to the next due date.

The next 60 days

A calendar and list of every charge and bill coming up, with totals for this week and the rest of the month.

Insights worth a look

Price increases, free trials that turned paid, overlapping services, missed or late payments, cards that expire before the next charge, renewals coming up, and anything marked cancelled that is still being charged. Snooze or dismiss each one.

Reminders on the phone

A local notification a few days before each charge or bill, an earlier notice for yearly renewals, and a nudge on the due date for bills you pay by hand. Amounts stay hidden on the lock screen.

Budgets with income

Monthly limits per category with progress bars, an income line and what is left after spending. Card payments and transfers between your own accounts are never counted twice.

A cancel helper

For each subscription: how to cancel, a link, and a checklist so you remember to save the confirmation and check the next statement.

Statement import

Bring in CSV, OFX, QFX or QIF files from your bank or card. Common layouts are recognized automatically, and for anything else you match the columns once. Duplicates are skipped.

Finds what repeats

It spots recurring charges and suggests new subscriptions, missing amounts, price changes and last-charged dates. You accept or reject each suggestion; nothing is added on its own.

Share or Open with

Send a backup or statement to the app from email, a file manager or a chat, or use Open with. You always see a preview first and choose Merge or Replace.

Locked and encrypted

Everything is encrypted on the phone with a key kept in the Android Keystore. Add an optional app lock with a PIN or your fingerprint, and hide the app from the recent-apps screen.

No internet permission

The app can't connect to anything: no accounts, no bank logins, no cloud and no tracking. You decide which files go in.

Screenshots

Tap any screenshot to enlarge it. They're shown in dark mode, the default; “Light mode” under a screenshot opens the light version. Every screenshot uses made-up demo data: fictional services, amounts and a pretend card.
