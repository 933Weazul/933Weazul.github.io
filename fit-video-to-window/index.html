Fit Video to Window

Full screen is too much and the little player is too small? Fit Video to Window makes the video on the page fill the browser window instead. Your tabs, address bar and taskbar stay where they are. Click the toolbar button or press Alt+Shift+F, and press Esc when you're done.

Personal project · Not publicly available

What it does

It's not the browser's real full-screen mode: the video simply takes over the window, and everything goes back exactly as it was afterwards.

Fills the window

The video grows to the size of the browser window, with black bars where needed. Nothing is cropped.

A button and a shortcut

Click the toolbar button or press Alt+Shift+F to toggle. The shortcut can be changed in the browser's extension settings.

Esc puts it back

Press Esc, the shortcut or the button again, and the page returns exactly as it was, scroll position included.

Picks the right video

The one that's playing (the biggest if several are), or else the biggest video on screen. A small ON badge shows while it's active.

Keeps the player's controls

On YouTube the whole player is enlarged so its own buttons keep working; Video.js, JW Player, Plyr and MediaElement players too. Plain videos get the browser's own controls.

Private

No data is collected or sent anywhere. It needs access to all sites only because a video can be on any page.
