DeskLink

Long message to write on your phone? Type it on your computer instead. DeskLink turns a browser tab on your PC into a keyboard, and optionally a mouse, for your Android phone. You don't install anything on the computer, and there's no cable or account.

Personal project · Not publicly available yet

How it works

Start a session on the phone, open the address it shows in a browser on your PC, and scan the QR code with the phone. When both screens show the same 6-digit code, confirm on the phone, and you're typing.

Real typing, from a real keyboard

Letters, accents, emoji, other languages and IME composition. Enter, Backspace, Delete, Tab, arrows, Home and End, plus Ctrl+A, C, V, X and Z.

Paste from your PC

Got text on the computer already? Drop it in the Paste from PC box and send it to the phone in one go.

Optional mouse mode

A small pointer follows your PC mouse on the phone. Click to tap, hold to drag, scroll with the wheel, and use shortcuts for Back, Home and Recents.

Quick, careful pairing

A one-time QR code that expires in about two minutes, a 6-digit code to compare on both screens, and Confirm on the phone. You can also type the code if the camera is a hassle.

Private by design

One computer at a time. Everything after pairing is encrypted and signed, and old messages can't be replayed. No internet use, no analytics and no accounts.

Only while you use it

The phone only listens while a session is running and the "session active" notification is showing (with a Stop button). It stops after 10 idle minutes.

A guide that walks you through it

A six-step setup guide with simple drawings, built into both the app and the web page, with directions for Pixel and Samsung phones.

Dark by default

Both the phone keyboard and the web page come in dark and light themes.

Screenshots

Tap any screenshot to enlarge it. They're shown in dark mode, the default; “Light mode” under a screenshot opens the light version. The home screen behind the pointer is a drawn stand-in.

DeskLink is a personal project and isn't publicly available yet. Mouse mode uses Android's accessibility features only to perform taps and swipes you make with your PC mouse. It doesn't read what's on the screen.
