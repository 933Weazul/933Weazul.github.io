Built for life on Wrangell Island, Alaska

Wrangell Daily

When the ferry comes in, whether the flight's on time, when the barge lands, how high the tide gets, and whether the fog will lift. All in one place, all in Alaska time.

Personal project · Not publicly available yet

A day on the island, in one app

Life in Southeast Alaska runs on boats, planes, tides and weather. Wrangell Daily keeps an eye on all of them, and you can pick which tabs you want. There may be a few surprises hidden in the app.

Ferries and barges

The next northbound and southbound ferry with delays and the boat's position, plus when the AML and Samson barges are due.

Your flights

Pick up to five flights by number or by route between your airports. See scheduled and estimated times, gates and delays from public flight-status pages, every leg even when a flight skips Wrangell, and swipe to tomorrow. Delay alerts stop once the flight has left Wrangell (or a stop you pick), which also saves requests.

Tides you can feel

A 48-hour tide curve painted like a watercolor, with highs and lows marked. Watch for the occasional fish or squid.

Weather with icons

Current conditions, the next 12 hours, the multi-day forecast and the local marine zones, each with a hand-drawn icon.

Aviation weather, in plain English

Decoded METARs and TAFs for up to five Southeast Alaska airports, with color-coded visibility and ceilings that use symbols as well as color.

Shopping, both directions

Southbound and Northbound lists, one per trip if you like. An experimental calculator compares checked bags with air cargo (estimates, not quotes).

Countdowns and ferry reminders

Count down to the next trip, pin your favorite, and get a reminder before a ferry sailing you picked from the schedule.

Daylight

Experimental: sunrise, sunset, twilight and the minutes gained or lost each day, worked out on the phone. Swipe through yesterday, today and tomorrow on a 24-hour light bar dimmed by the cloud and rain forecast.

Fishing and boating openings

Experimental and off until you turn it on: openings, closures and emergency orders for the Wrangell and Stikine area from ADF&G's public pages, once a day at most. Not official; always check ADF&G.

An optional Orca Sunset theme

A warm sunset title bar, layered blue mountains, spruce silhouettes and striped water with a couple of orca fins. Original art; Classic stays the default, and text keeps WCAG AA contrast in light and dark.

Home-screen widgets

Next flight, the tide, flight categories and a countdown at a glance. They use what the app already has, so they add no extra requests.

Power and water notices

Outage and boil-water notices from local sources, with optional notifications.

Works when the signal doesn't

Every tab keeps its last copy and says how old it is. Pull down to refresh when you're back in range.

Screenshots

Tap any screenshot to enlarge it. Shown in dark mode; “Light mode” under a screenshot opens the light version. Most show public data captured on Oct 17, 2026; anything else is marked as example data.
