Phone Gallery

Your phone's photos and videos on the big screen. Start it on the phone, open the address in a browser on your computer, TV or tablet, and wander through every folder. No cables, cloud or accounts.

Personal project · Not publicly available yet

What it does

The phone runs a small password-protected gallery on your own Wi-Fi. Everything stays at home.

Every folder, beautifully

Camera, downloads, screenshots and the SD card in a clean photo grid. Sort it, filter it as you type, and switch between light and dark.

A proper photo viewer

Swipe or use the arrow keys, zoom and pan, check the details, and rotate without losing quality.

Timeline

Everything on the phone grouped by month. Drag the side scrubber to jump back years in a second.

Slideshows and video

Fade or Ken Burns slideshows with optional music. Videos pick up where you stopped, with speed control and frame stepping.

TV mode

Open the gallery in the TV's browser, pair it with a short code, and drive it from your laptop. TV remotes work too.

Duplicate finder

Finds exact copies and look-alike photos. It always asks before deleting and always keeps the last copy.

Upload and download

Drag files onto the page to put them on the phone, or pick several photos and grab them as one .zip.

Private by design

A password you choose, on your home network only. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Screenshots

Tap any screenshot to enlarge it. All pictures shown are generated sample images and test videos.
