Phone Trackpad

Sitting back from the screen, or the laptop's touchpad is acting up? Phone Trackpad turns your Android phone into a trackpad and a keyboard for your Windows PC. A small app on the PC shows a QR code; scan it once with the phone, and from then on the phone reconnects by itself. No cable, Bluetooth, cloud or account.

Personal project · Not publicly available yet

How it works

Start the small Phone Trackpad window on the PC and open the app on the phone. Scan the QR code in the window once, and the phone is a trackpad. Tap the keyboard button to type.

A real trackpad

One finger moves the pointer and a tap clicks. Tap, then tap and drag, to drag. Big Left and Right buttons, and a pointer speed setting.

Gestures you know

Two fingers scroll up, down and sideways, a two-finger tap right-clicks, and a three-finger tap middle-clicks.

Type with your phone keyboard

New in 1.2.0. Suggestions, autocorrect and swipe typing all work. Each word goes to the PC once it's finished, so the PC gets the corrected word. Any language and emoji arrive as they are.

Shortcuts and special keys

Ctrl, Alt, Shift and Win apply to the next key, so Ctrl then c is Ctrl+C. Rows of Esc, Tab, arrows, Home and End, Delete, F1 to F12, plus Copy, Paste, Undo, Alt+Tab and Show desktop.

Send a block of text

Type or paste a longer piece of text on the phone and send it in one go. New lines arrive as Enter.

Private typing

A Private switch turns off the phone keyboard's suggestions and learning, for passwords.

Pair once with a QR code

A one-time code that's used up after pairing. No camera? Type the short code shown under it instead. After that the phone reconnects on its own.

Encrypted on your Wi-Fi

Each phone gets its own key, every connection gets a fresh one, and all input is encrypted and checked. Messages from unpaired phones, forged or replayed ones are dropped. Nothing goes to the internet.

A tiny Windows app

One small window, no installer. Keys arrive as real key presses, so shortcuts work across apps. Windows doesn't let it type into administrator windows or UAC prompts.

Dark by default

The phone app follows your phone's dark or light setting.

Get the PC app from the phone

New in 1.3.0. The matching Windows app is built into the phone app. Turn on Share Windows app and open the address it shows in the PC's browser to download it, with its version and checksum shown. Off by default, local network only, and it stops after 10 minutes.

Screenshots

Tap any screenshot to enlarge it. They're shown in dark mode; “Light mode” under a screenshot opens the light version. The PC name and all text are made up, and the phone's own keyboard isn't drawn. Screens that show addresses, codes or QR codes are left out, and on the Share Windows app screen the address is a placeholder and the QR code is blurred.

Phone Trackpad is a personal project and isn't publicly available yet. It works on your local network only and isn't affiliated with Microsoft or Google. Windows doesn't accept input from apps like this one in windows running as administrator, in UAC prompts or on the lock screen.
