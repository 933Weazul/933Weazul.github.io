Small apps for everyday island life

Four home-made Android apps from Wrangell Island in Southeast Alaska. One makes a phone's photos easy to enjoy on the big screen, one gathers ferries, flights, tides and weather in one place, one lets you type on your phone from your computer, and one keeps an eye on subscriptions and bills.

The apps

All four are personal projects and aren't publicly available yet. These pages are a look at what they do.

Phone Gallery

Browse every photo and video on your phone from any browser on your home Wi-Fi. Timeline, slideshows, a TV mode and a duplicate finder.

See Phone Gallery →

Wrangell Daily

Ferries, flights, barges, tides, weather, aviation weather, shopping lists, countdowns, daylight and local alerts, plus home-screen widgets, all in Alaska time.

See Wrangell Daily →

DeskLink

Use your computer's keyboard, and optionally its mouse, to type on and control your Android phone. Pair in seconds over Wi-Fi with a QR code; no PC app needed.

See DeskLink →

Tidewater Money

A private, offline tracker for subscriptions and bills: what renews when, price increases, trials that turned paid, reminders, budgets and statement import. Everything stays encrypted on the phone.

See Tidewater Money →
