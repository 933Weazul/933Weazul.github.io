Small apps for everyday island life

Six home-made Android apps from Wrangell Island in Southeast Alaska. One makes a phone's photos easy to enjoy on the big screen, one gathers ferries, flights, tides and weather in one place, one lets you type on your phone from your computer, one keeps an eye on subscriptions and bills, one turns your phone into a trackpad and keyboard for a Windows PC, and one keeps the others up to date.

The apps

All six are personal projects and aren't publicly available yet. These pages are a look at what they do.

Phone Gallery

Browse every photo and video on your phone from any browser on your home Wi-Fi. Timeline, slideshows, a TV mode and a duplicate finder.

See Phone Gallery →

Wrangell Daily

Ferries, flights, barges, tides, weather, aviation weather, shopping lists, countdowns, daylight and local alerts, plus home-screen widgets, all in Alaska time.

See Wrangell Daily →

DeskLink

Use your computer's keyboard, and optionally its mouse, to type on and control your Android phone. Swipe and voice typing on the phone too.

See DeskLink →

Tidewater Money

A private, offline tracker for subscriptions and bills: what renews when, price increases, trials that turned paid, reminders, budgets and statement import. Everything stays encrypted on the phone.

See Tidewater Money →

Phone Trackpad

Your phone as a wireless trackpad and keyboard for a Windows PC: move, click and scroll, type with the phone keyboard, and send shortcuts like Ctrl+C and Alt+Tab. Paired once with a QR code, encrypted on your Wi-Fi.

See Phone Trackpad →

App Hub

Keeps the other apps up to date: installed vs latest version, release notes, a daily check and one-tap updates through Android's own Install prompt. Every download's signature is checked first.

See App Hub →

PC tools

Small helpers for a Windows PC and the Edge browser, made for everyday use at home. Like the apps, they're personal projects and aren't publicly available.

Fit Video to Window

An Edge extension that makes the video on a page fill the browser window: like full screen, but your tabs and taskbar stay put. Toolbar button or Alt+Shift+F, Esc to exit.

See Fit Video to Window →

Convert WebP to JPEG

Right-click a .webp file in File Explorer and choose Convert to JPEG. The .jpg lands next to it, and the original is left alone.

See Convert WebP to JPEG →

Cast to Roku

A desktop button that opens the Windows Cast panel and connects to the Roku by itself. One double-click instead of Win+K and hunting for the TV.

See Cast to Roku →
