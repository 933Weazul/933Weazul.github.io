Small apps for everyday island life

Two home-made Android apps from Wrangell Island in Southeast Alaska. One makes a phone's photos easy to enjoy on the big screen. The other gathers ferries, flights, tides and weather in one place.

The apps

Both are personal projects and aren't publicly available yet. These pages are a look at what they do.

Phone Gallery

Browse every photo and video on your phone from any browser on your home Wi-Fi. Timeline, slideshows, a TV mode and a duplicate finder.

See Phone Gallery →

Wrangell Daily

Ferries, flights, barges, tides, weather, aviation weather, shopping lists, countdowns, daylight and local alerts, plus home-screen widgets, all in Alaska time.
